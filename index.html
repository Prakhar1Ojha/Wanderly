7 days across Kerala

Duration 7 days
Nights 6 nights
From ₹34,900
Rating ★ 4.9
Day 01 — Kochi
Arrive in Kochi. The evening walk through Fort Kochi — the Chinese fishing nets, the old Dutch cemetery, the synagogue lane — takes an hour and tells you everything about why this city exists where it does. Dinner at a homestay table, not a restaurant.
Day 02 — Munnar
Drive up into the Cardamom Hills. The road itself is worth the journey — waterfalls, rubber plantations and a temperature drop of ten degrees. Arrive at your estate stay by mid-afternoon. The afternoon mist makes everything quiet.
Day 03 — Tea Country
Up before the buses. Walk the estate paths in the early light. Visit the tea factory to understand what you've been drinking your whole life. Top Station in the afternoon if the cloud cooperates — 1880 metres, Tamil Nadu laid out below.
Day 04 — Alleppey
Board a kettuvallam at Alleppey. For the next 24 hours, time moves at the speed of the water. Cook on the boat. Sleep to the sound of nothing happening. This is the only correct way to do the backwaters.
Day 05 — Varkala
Drive south to Varkala. The cliff beach is one of the few in Kerala that still feels like itself. Spend the afternoon in the water. The tandoori grilled fish at the cliff shacks is real, not tourist-facing. Walk the cliff path at sunset before dinner.
Day 06 — The Coast
Drive down to Kovalam or Poovar. The lagoon at Poovar Island is accessible only by boat — which means it's never too crowded. Ayurvedic treatment if you want it. The kind of evening where you don't need to make a plan.
Day 07 — Departure
Trivandrum airport is 30 minutes away. There's time for a slow breakfast, a last walk on the beach, and the kind of quiet regret that means you did something right.
